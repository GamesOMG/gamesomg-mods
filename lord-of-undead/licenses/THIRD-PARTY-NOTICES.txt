GamesOMG Teleport for Lord of Undead: third-party software in the Easy install zip

The Easy install zip carries the BepInEx 6 mod loader (IL2CPP build) exactly as its authors publish it, every file of its
zip, unmodified, from:
  BepInEx-Unity.IL2CPP-win-x64-6.0.0-be.793+dbc82f0.zip
  https://builds.bepinex.dev/projects/bepinex_be (build 793, commit dbc82f05b8ea34833de5b6a723eb60dd8398e0b1)
  SHA-256 5b63d7d0e92a2448ccd13b738ff95ff1cc1026c6d718555d4df8e6c824953ac4

Component, the files it covers, and where its source and licence are. Each licence's own text is in this folder.

BepInEx 6.0.0-be.793 (the loader)
  BepInEx/core/BepInEx.*.dll and their .xml files, changelog.txt
  BepInEx-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx (commit dbc82f0)

UnityDoorstop (starts BepInEx with the game)
  winhttp.dll, doorstop_config.ini, .doorstop_version
  UnityDoorstop-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://github.com/NeighTools/UnityDoorstop

Il2CppInterop (lets a mod talk to an IL2CPP game)
  BepInEx/core/Il2CppInterop.*.dll
  Il2CppInterop-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://github.com/BepInEx/Il2CppInterop

Cpp2IL and LibCpp2IL (read the game's code layout on the first start)
  BepInEx/core/Cpp2IL.Core.dll, LibCpp2IL.dll, Disarm.dll, WasmDisassembler.dll, StableNameDotNet.dll
  Cpp2IL-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://github.com/SamboyCoding/Cpp2IL

HarmonyX, MonoMod (method patching)
  BepInEx/core/0Harmony.dll, MonoMod.*.dll
  HarmonyX-LICENSE.txt, MonoMod-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://github.com/BepInEx/HarmonyX, https://github.com/MonoMod/MonoMod

Dobby (native hooks)
  BepInEx/core/dobby.dll
  Dobby-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://github.com/jmpews/Dobby

The .NET 6 runtime BepInEx runs on
  dotnet/
  dotnet-runtime-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime

Also in BepInEx/core: AsmResolver, AssetRipper.CIL and AssetRipper.Primitives, Iced, Gee.External.Capstone, Mono.Cecil,
SemanticVersioning, each under its own licence as published by its authors (see each project's repository).

The mod itself (BepInEx/plugins/GamesOMGTeleport/) is MIT licensed: LICENSE.txt beside the DLL.
